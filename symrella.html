SYMRELLA is a private, local-first cycle observation app designed to help you record temperature, cervical mucus, bleeding and other daily observations in one place.

Open SYMRELLA No account is required to start using the web app.

Daily observations

Record basal temperature, cervical mucus, bleeding and relevant context with a simple daily interface.

Charts & history

Review your observations through charts, calendar views and cycle history.

Learn

Explore educational modules covering cycle observations, chart reading, thermal shifts and particular situations.

Designed for privacy

Your data stays on your device. SYMRELLA is designed as a local-first web application. Your observations are stored in your browser rather than sent to a SYMRELLA server. The app also provides data export and import tools so you can keep control of your information.

SYMRELLA is an observation and educational tool. It does not diagnose medical conditions or replace advice from a qualified healthcare professional.

Available internationally

The application interface supports multiple languages, including English, French, Spanish and Arabic. Temperature display can also be configured according to the available settings.
